*Potato dumplings with Bryndza,fried onion, bacon and fresh spring onion*

STEPS (youtube tutorial)

  1. Cut the bacon and onion into a small pieces and fry them on the butter/pig fat (a.k.a Lard) and then place them aside for a later serving.
  2. Grate the potatoes to a mushy consistency-either with mixer or handy grater.
  3. Add flour and egg to a mushy potatoes and incorporate it well.If i
  4. Add approximately 2 liters of water to a pot and add 1-2 tbsp of salt, bring it to a boiling temperature.
  5. Once the water is boiling hot, hold Speatlze Maker above the water and push potato mix through holes to the water.
  6. Wait till dumplings flows up to the surface and let it cook approx. 3-5 more minutes and then scoop them out(If they dont flow up to the surface after a while, just scoop them out anyway). I personally like to run hot water over those scooped dumplings so I get rid of that slimy film that sticks on them.
  7. Put your cooked dumplings to a bowl and mix it with Bryndza cheese and add fried bacon with fried golden onion and top it with fresh spring onion!

You will need:

    -Ingredients

  • butter/cooking fat(for frying part)
  • 1kg potatoes
  • 300g semi-coarse wheat flour
  • 300g of fatty bacon
  • 350g Bryndza cheese
  • sour cream 16%
  • salt (1-2 tbsp)
  • 1 egg
  • random amount of spring onion
  • (optional) onion

    -Accessories

  • 1x 3-5 liter cooking pot
  • 1x frying pan
  • 1x handy grater or mixer
  • 1x Stainless Steel Spaetzle Maker
  • 1x noodle strainer
  • 1x Stainless Steel Foam Spoon
