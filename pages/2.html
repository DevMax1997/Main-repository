*Beetroot risotto*

STEPS (youtube tutorial)

  1. cut celery and onion into small pieces , put it in a pan with lard/fat, bay leafs,salt and pepper and slowly let it fry on low heat.
  2. When onion and celery get soft/onion glossy yellow. => turn on high heat and immediately put the rice with fresh thyme in and start stiring the rice, once it starts to get sticky,immediately pour white dry/semidry wine into it, let it reduce. Once the wine is gone, immediately pour vegetable stock in(not all the stock) and continuously add the stock while mixing until the rice is done.The point is to not to let the rice get stuck on the pan, and let it cook in the stock.
  3. Once the rice is Al dente, put the grated beetroot into and mix it well and let it finish. Once the rice is done, turn off the heat.
  4. The moment rice is done, we add butter, grated parmezan cheese and lemon juice and if needed for proper texture, add desired amount of additional stock and serve !!!
  5. Serve with basil/arugula on top, torched goat cheese on side along with caramelized honey walnuts and little splash of extra virgin olive on top.

You will need :

-Ingredients

  • butter
  • lard/ beef tallow
  • celery
  • rice
  • onion
  • bay leaf
  • salt
  • pepper
  • fresh thyme
  • fresh beetroot
  • white dry/semidry wine
  • vegetable stock
  • parmigiano reggiano
  • goat cheese
  • arugula/parsley
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • walnuts
  • honey
